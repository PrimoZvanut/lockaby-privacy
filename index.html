Lockaby

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 24, 2026

Lockaby does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works on your device and has no servers of its own and no user accounts. Its only network use is Google Play's billing service, which checks whether you have purchased the full version.

Who we are

Lockaby ("the app") is developed by Primož Žvanut ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the Android application published under the package name com.khey.pz.lockaby.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. Lockaby does not gather personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or any other personal or sensitive data.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, and no advertising library.

Information stored on your device

Lockaby saves a small amount of configuration locally so the app remembers your setup between sessions. This includes:

  • Which app you selected to lock
  • Your preference settings within the app
  • Whether you have already seen the one-time onboarding instructions
  • How many of your free locks you have used
  • Whether you have purchased the full version

This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to our servers because we do not operate any servers. Uninstalling Lockaby permanently deletes it.

Purchases

Lockaby is free to install and includes ten free locks. After those are used, a single one-time purchase removes the limit permanently. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play, which acts as the seller. Lockaby never sees your name, card number, billing address, or any other payment detail. It receives only a confirmation that the purchase exists, and stores that single fact on your device.

Google's handling of the transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions and why they are needed

Lockaby requests the minimum permissions required for it to function:

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — This is the core function of the app. It allows Lockaby to place a protective lock screen on top of the app your child is using, so they cannot navigate away or tap other controls. The overlay is only shown when you activate it, and it is removed when you unlock.
  • Foreground service — Keeps the lock active reliably while your screen is locked, and prevents Android from shutting the lock down unexpectedly.
  • Notifications — Shows an ongoing notice while the lock is active, and tells you when your free locks have run out.
  • Internet and network state — Declared by Google's billing library so the app can confirm your purchase with Google Play. Lockaby opens no connections of its own and sends no data anywhere.
  • In-app billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — Enables the one-time purchase through Google Play.

To let you choose which app to lock, Lockaby asks Android for the list of apps that have a launcher icon. That list is read on your device at the moment you open the picker, is never stored, and is never transmitted.

Lockaby does not use an Accessibility Service and does not request Device Owner privileges.

Screen content and audio

Lockaby does not record, capture, screenshot, or transmit anything shown on your screen. To automatically release the lock when a phone call arrives, the app checks whether the device's ringer is currently active. It cannot access call content, phone numbers, contacts, or the microphone.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any data to share. Lockaby has no advertising partners and no analytics processors.

The only third party involved is Google Play, and only as the processor of your purchase.

Children's privacy

Lockaby is a parental control tool intended to be installed and configured by a parent or guardian on their own device. The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no data in our possession that could be exposed in a breach. All settings remain in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's standard app sandbox.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Lockaby introduces features that require handling data differently, we will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above before releasing that version.
